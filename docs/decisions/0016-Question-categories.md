# Question Categories

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | Information that directly identifies or distinguishes the character as an individual. |
| **Media** | The type of media or medium in which the character primarily appears, such as anime, manga, movie, TV series, video game, comic, or novel. |
| **Universe** | The fictional universe, franchise, series, or world that the character belongs to. |
| **Role** | The character's narrative, organizational, or functional role within their story, such as protagonist, antagonist, hero, villain, mentor, or leader. |
| **Species** | The character's biological, supernatural, mechanical, or existential type, such as human, alien, robot, demon, or animal. |
| **Abilities** | The character's powers, skills, capabilities, or special traits that determine what they can do. |
| **Appearance** | The character's observable physical and visual characteristics, such as hair color, clothing, body features, or distinctive marks. |
| **Relationships** | The character's connections with other characters, groups, or entities, such as siblings, parents, rivals, partners, mentors, or friends. |
